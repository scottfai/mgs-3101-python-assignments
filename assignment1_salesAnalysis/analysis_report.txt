Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

Dataset Overview
The dataset contains coffee shop transactions, including products sold,
sales quantities, pricing information, dates, times, and store locations.

Key Findings
1. Coffee beverages generate most of the shop's revenue.
2. Peak sales occur during morning hours.
3. Some pastry products do not sell as often as other drinks.

Recommendations
1. Increase staffing and inventory during peak hours.
2. Endorse low-selling pastries with combo deals.
3. Focus advertising on the highest-revenue drink products.

Questions
1. Why do some store places perform better than others?
2. Which seasonal trends are in customer consumption?
3. Does a loyalty rewards program increase continuous visits?
